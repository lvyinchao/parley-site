SyncTalk 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日

SyncTalk（部分版本以 Parley 名称发布，以下称“本应用”）是一款实时同声传译工具。我们重视你的隐私，本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。

我们不收集你的个人信息

本应用没有账号系统，不包含任何广告、统计分析或追踪 SDK。开发者不运营任何服务器，不会收集、存储或查看你的语音、文字、联系方式或设备标识。

麦克风与语音数据

你开始翻译后，本应用通过麦克风采集语音，并经加密连接（WSS/TLS）实时发送到你在设置中选择的翻译服务（千问AI平台或阿里云百炼），用于语音识别、翻译和生成译文语音。本应用不保存录音。字幕默认只保留在运行中的应用内存中；停止翻译后仍可查看，清空字幕或应用退出后不再保留。

在支持此功能的版本中，你可以主动选择将字幕保存到本机，并在应用中删除；也可以通过系统分享功能导出。应用将保存的字幕排除在设备备份之外。导出后，副本由你选择的接收应用或服务处理。已保存的字幕不会因清空当前字幕而自动删除。

语音数据由翻译服务提供方依据其自身的隐私政策处理，请参阅：千问AI平台、阿里云。

API Key

你填写的 API Key 和 Workspace ID 保存在设备钥匙串（Keychain）中，用于连接你选择的翻译服务及进行身份验证，不会发送给开发者或其他第三方。钥匙串项目可能包含在系统加密备份中，并可在恢复备份时迁移到新设备。

本地偏好设置

目标语言、音色、输入设备等偏好设置保存在设备本地，仅用于记住你的选择。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更与联系方式

如本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面并标注最新更新日期。如有任何问题，请联系：lvyinchao@gmail.com。

SyncTalk Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

SyncTalk (published as Parley in some versions, “the app”) is a real-time interpretation tool. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

We don't collect your personal data

The app has no accounts and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The developer runs no servers and does not collect, store, or view your speech, text, contact details, or device identifiers.

Microphone and speech data

When you start translating, the app captures speech from the microphone and streams it over an encrypted connection (WSS/TLS) to the translation service you selected in Settings (Qianwen AI Platform or Alibaba Cloud Model Studio) for speech recognition, translation, and speech synthesis. The app does not save recordings. Captions remain only in the running app's memory by default: they remain visible after stopping, and are removed when you clear them or the app exits.

In versions that support this feature, you can choose to save captions on your device and delete them in the app, or export them using the system share sheet. The app excludes saved captions from device backups. An exported copy is handled by the app or service you choose. Clearing current captions does not automatically delete saved copies.

Speech data is processed by the translation provider under its own privacy policy: Qianwen AI Platform, Alibaba Cloud.

API key

The API key and Workspace ID you enter are stored in your device's Keychain and used to connect and authenticate with the translation service you chose. They are never sent to the developer or any other third party. Keychain items may be included in encrypted system backups and migrated to a new device when a backup is restored.

Local preferences

Preferences such as target language, voice, and input device are stored locally on your device only to remember your choices.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its last-updated date. Questions? Contact lvyinchao@gmail.com.
