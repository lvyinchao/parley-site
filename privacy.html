Parley 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 16 日

Parley（以下称“本应用”）是一款实时同声传译工具。我们重视你的隐私，本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。

我们不收集你的个人信息

本应用没有账号系统，不包含任何广告、统计分析或追踪 SDK。开发者不运营任何服务器，不会收集、存储或查看你的语音、文字、联系方式或设备标识。

麦克风与语音数据

你开始翻译后，本应用通过麦克风采集语音，并经加密连接（WSS/TLS）实时发送到你在设置中选择的翻译服务（千问AI平台或阿里云百炼），用于语音识别、翻译和生成译文语音。翻译结果仅在本机显示和播放；停止翻译或清空字幕后，本应用不会保存任何录音或字幕。

语音数据由翻译服务提供方依据其自身的隐私政策处理，请参阅：千问AI平台、阿里云。

API Key

你填写的 API Key 和 Workspace ID 仅保存在设备本地的钥匙串（Keychain）中，只用于向你选择的翻译服务进行身份验证，不会发送给开发者或其他任何第三方。

本地偏好设置

目标语言、音色、输入设备等偏好设置保存在设备本地，仅用于记住你的选择。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更与联系方式

如本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期。如有任何问题，请联系：lvyinchao@gmail.com。

Parley Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Parley (“the app”) is a real-time interpretation tool. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

We don't collect your personal data

The app has no accounts and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The developer runs no servers and does not collect, store, or view your speech, text, contact details, or device identifiers.

Microphone and speech data

When you start translating, the app captures speech from the microphone and streams it over an encrypted connection (WSS/TLS) to the translation service you selected in Settings (Qianwen AI Platform or Alibaba Cloud Model Studio) for speech recognition, translation, and speech synthesis. Results are only displayed and played on your device; the app keeps no recordings or captions after you stop or clear them.

Speech data is processed by the translation provider under its own privacy policy: Qianwen AI Platform, Alibaba Cloud.

API key

The API key and Workspace ID you enter are stored only in your device's Keychain and are used only to authenticate with the translation service you chose. They are never sent to the developer or any other third party.

Local preferences

Preferences such as target language, voice, and input device are stored locally on your device only to remember your choices.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions? Contact lvyinchao@gmail.com.
